Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

The short version

  • Your library is stored in your browser on your device. The PT Atlas maintainer never receives it.
  • There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, cookies for tracking, or third-party trackers.
  • Google features are optional. If you use them, PT Atlas reads only the Google Docs you pick and keeps a sync copy only in a hidden folder in your own Google Drive.
  • Do not enter patient-identifying information. PT Atlas is not designed or offered for protected health information.

1. Who we are

PT Atlas is a free, open web application for organizing a personal library of physical therapy interventions, conditions, protocols and notes. It is published at teprice92.github.io/pt-atlas by the PT Atlas maintainer ("we", "us"). Its source code is public at github.com/teprice92/pt-atlas.

2. Information stored on your device

Everything you add to PT Atlas, including entries, conditions, protocol phases, collections, attachments, revision history and imported document text, is stored in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB) on the device you are using. Small preferences, such as your theme, sidebar state and an optional Google project configuration, are stored in your browser's localStorage. This information stays on your device. PT Atlas has no server that collects it, and we cannot see, access or recover it.

The app also caches its own files in your browser so it works offline. That cache contains only the application, not your library.

3. Hosting

PT Atlas is served by GitHub Pages. When your browser downloads the app, GitHub receives standard request information, such as your IP address and browser details, and may keep it in security logs as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not receive or use these logs. PT Atlas uses no third-party fonts, scripts, analytics or advertising.

4. Google features (optional)

You can use PT Atlas without a Google account. If you choose to connect Google, the app uses Google's sign-in in your browser and asks only for the permissions needed for the feature you use:

PermissionWhen it is requestedWhat PT Atlas does with it
drive.file
Files you choose		When you choose Choose Google Docs or Check imported Docs for updatesReads the name, modified time and text of the Google Docs you select in Google's file picker, so you can review them and import them into your library, and later check those same documents for changes. PT Atlas cannot see any other files in your Drive and never edits, moves or deletes your documents.
drive.appdata
Hidden app folder		Only when you turn on Sync across devicesStores a copy of your library and its attachments in a hidden folder in your own Google Drive that only PT Atlas can access, and reads it back on your other devices to keep them in sync. It cannot access your other Drive files.

All Google requests go directly from your browser to Google. The data never passes through a PT Atlas server, because there isn't one. Google access tokens are kept only in your browser's memory for the current session. They are never written to storage, included in backups or sent anywhere else.

Limited Use. PT Atlas's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, Google user data is used only to provide the import and sync features you request in the app. It is not sold, not transferred to anyone else, not used for advertising, not used to determine credit-worthiness or for lending, and not used to develop, improve or train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models. No person reads your Google data.

You can disconnect at any time from Settings. To revoke PT Atlas's access completely, visit your Google Account's third-party connections. To delete the synced copy, use Remove synced copy from Google Drive in Settings, or delete PT Atlas's hidden app data under Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps.

5. Import and label matching

When you import pasted text, a Word document or a Google Doc, PT Atlas processes it entirely in your browser. The optional "fill fields from labels" feature is simple pattern matching performed on your device. No artificial intelligence or external service is involved, and nothing is sent anywhere.

6. Patient information

PT Atlas is a reference library for clinical knowledge. It is not a medical record and is not intended for protected health information (PHI) or any information that identifies a patient. Do not enter or attach patient names, dates of birth, record numbers, images or other identifiers. We are not a covered entity or business associate under HIPAA and do not sign business associate agreements.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data remains until you delete it. You can delete entries in the app, clear PT Atlas's site data in your browser settings (which permanently removes the local library unless you have a backup or sync copy), and remove the Google Drive sync copy as described above. Backups you export are files you control. Because we do not hold your data, we cannot delete or recover it for you.

8. Security

PT Atlas is served only over HTTPS. Imported files are processed with size limits and attachments are checked by their content, and the app never runs scripts from documents. Your library's safety also depends on your device and browser: use a device lock, keep your browser updated, and export backups regularly.

9. Children

PT Atlas is intended for licensed clinicians and students in professional programs. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. The full history of changes is visible in the public repository.

11. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: please open an issue at github.com/teprice92/pt-atlas/issues. Do not include patient information or other sensitive details in an issue.
